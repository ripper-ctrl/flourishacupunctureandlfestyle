FAQ

Questions & Answers

What is Neuro-Acupuncture?

Neuro-Acupuncture is an advanced form of acupuncture that targets specific neurological points to stimulate the nervous system. It combines traditional acupuncture with modern neuroscience to treat conditions like chronic pain, neurological disorders, and brain-related conditions.

Do you offer home visits?

Yes, Dr. Ogunjebe provides home assessment and clinical evaluation services. This allows patients to receive care in the comfort and convenience of their own environment, which can be particularly beneficial for those with mobility issues or complex conditions.

How long does a typical session last?

A typical session lasts between 45–60 minutes, depending on the complexity of your condition and the treatment plan. Initial consultations may be longer to allow for comprehensive assessment.

Is acupuncture painful?

Most patients experience minimal discomfort during acupuncture treatments. The needles used are very thin and insertion is typically painless or causes only a slight sensation. Dr. Ogunjebe ensures patient comfort throughout the treatment process.

What conditions can you treat?

Dr. Ogunjebe treats a wide range of conditions including chronic pain, migraines, neurological disorders, fertility issues, menstrual disorders, stress-related conditions, and general wellness concerns.

How do I prepare for my first appointment?

For your first appointment, please bring any relevant medical records, imaging results, or previous treatment information. Wear comfortable clothing and ensure you've eaten a light meal beforehand. Dr. Ogunjebe will provide specific preparation instructions when confirming your appointment.
